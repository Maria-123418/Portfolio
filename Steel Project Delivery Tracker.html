1 · Problem

Steel Project Delivery Tracker

Steel delivery delays create uncertainty for project customers.

🏗️ Site crews and erection schedules depend on arrival dates
⏳ A delay with no explanation feels worse than the delay itself
📉 Silence reduces trust, even when the cause is outside the company's control
2 · Customer pain point

"Where is my material?"

Customers don't know the delivery status, so they call or message repeatedly.

Customer waits, no update
↓
Calls the company for status
↓
Team chases logistics, calls back
↓
Repeat tomorrow

Result: wasted staff time and a frustrated customer.

3 · Digital solution

5 · Expected benefit

Better communication, greater trust

Better communication→Less uncertainty→Better customer experience→Greater trust

Project objective

To improve customer communication and maintain trust during delivery delays through digital updates and delivery-status tracking.

Side benefit

Fewer repeat status calls, so the team spends less time chasing updates.
